这页在干嘛？——30 秒读懂What is this page? — read it in 30 seconds

「线性规划（LP）」就是：在一堆硬性要求（够咸、够鲜、营养达标、限量不超…）下，找出成本最低的配料方案。G1＝把方便面调料包的钠降下来，成本还要最低；G2＝把植物蛋白混到营养达标，成本还要最低。页面上的每个数字都是当场用精确分数算出来的（不是查表、不是估算）——拖动任何滑杆、改任何价格，立即重算。Linear programming means: given a stack of hard requirements (salty enough, savoury enough, nutrition on target, caps not exceeded…), find the cheapest possible blend. G1 = cut the sodium of an instant-noodle seasoning pack at minimum cost; G2 = blend plant proteins to nutrition targets at minimum cost. Every number on this page is solved on the spot with exact rational arithmetic — not a lookup table, not an estimate; move any slider or price and it re-solves instantly.

怎么读下面的表（速查）：How to read the tables below (quick guide):

  • 最优配方＝「省钱冠军」组合：满足全部要求、成本最低的用量（表里灰色行＝没被选用）；Optimal recipe = the champion-of-cheap blend: the usage meeting every requirement at the lowest cost (grey rows = not used);
  • 贴线／顶满＝刚好卡死的要求：最省方案正好把它用满，再严一点就得换料或涨价；binding = a requirement exactly maxed out: the cheapest plan touches it, any tighter and the recipe or price must change;
  • 影子价格＝这个要求「值多少钱」：放松 1 个单位能省多少成本（0＝还有富余，不卡成本）；shadow price = what the requirement is "worth": how much cost a one-unit relaxation saves (0 = slack remains, it does not bind);
  • 价格临界点＝涨跌的分水岭：某原料涨过／降到某个价，最省配方就换人；price breakpoint = the tipping point: above/below a certain price, the cheapest recipe swaps that ingredient;
  • 曲线／前沿＝要求强度与最低成本的关系：越陡＝这个要求越贵；curves / frontier = requirement strictness vs minimum cost: the steeper, the dearer the requirement;
  • 待标定＝文献典型值，还没做实验标定——看方向、看量级可以，别当实测值。to be calibrated = literature-typical value, not yet experimentally calibrated — fine for direction and magnitude, not measured truth.
更多术语（换料／有效区间／枢转／交替最优／AAS／λmax／晶体氨基酸／精确分数）More terms (swap / validity range / pivot / alternate optima / AAS / λmax / crystal AAs / exact rationals)

换料（换基）：最优解从一个顶点跳到相邻顶点，配方里的原料换人——曲线折点、区间端点都是它。swap (basis change): the optimum jumps to a neighbouring vertex and an ingredient is swapped out — curve bends and range endpoints are exactly these.

有效区间：影子价格在该区间内保持不变；越过端点就换料、价格跳档。validity range: the shadow price stays constant inside it; beyond an endpoint a swap happens and the price changes.

枢转：求解算法（单纯形法）的一次迭代步骤；「7 次枢转」＝这次求解走了 7 步。pivot: one iteration step of the simplex algorithm; "7 pivots" = the solve took 7 steps.

交替最优：最低成本唯一，但有多套同样便宜的配方（等价解，任选其一）。alternate optima: the minimum cost is unique but several equally cheap recipes exist (equivalent solutions).

AAS（氨基酸评分）：某个必需氨基酸「供给÷需求」；九项中最低的一项决定整条配方的蛋白质量。AAS (amino-acid score): supply ÷ demand for one essential amino acid; the lowest of the nine sets the protein quality of the whole recipe.

λmax：九项营养下限还能「整体同乘放大」的最大倍数——这套原料在口感上限内的质量天花板。λmax: the largest factor the nine nutrition floors can still be scaled by together — the quality ceiling of these ingredients within the sensory caps.

晶体氨基酸：直接添加的游离氨基酸粉（营养上的「捷径」项），算例设 ≤4g 上限。crystal amino acids: free amino-acid powders added directly (a nutritional "shortcut"), capped at 4g in the case.

精确分数：全 BigInt 分数运算，页面显示的是精确值四舍五入——与浮点求解器可能差在末位，属预期。exact rationals: BigInt fraction arithmetic throughout; displayed values are exact values rounded — last-digit differences vs floating-point solvers are expected.

咸度等效系数、酸提咸增益、苦味掩蔽与 I+G 协同有效系数均为「待标定」文献/示意系数（I+G 协同为线性化，仅典型配比附近有效）。调滑杆/开关即精确重解（分数运算，非查表）。

Saltiness coefficients, acid gain, bitterness masking and the I+G synergy coefficient are to-be-calibrated literature/placeholder values (I+G synergy linearized, valid near typical ratios). Every control re-solves exactly (rational arithmetic, not a lookup table).

口径：100g 调味料 LP 精确求解；按 8g 方便面调料包折算；数字为当前参数下的实时值（经典盘默认＝审计口径：+¥576 / +¥764 每吨 ≈ 4.6 / 6.1 厘/包）。

Caliber: exact LP solve per 100g seasoning; converted to 8g packets; values are live under current parameters (classic-palette defaults = audited caliber: +¥576 / +¥764 per ton ≈ 4.6 / 6.1 li per packet).

六项系数为文献典型值·待标定（每原料 data 文件 n6 字段），「GB 声称档」的糖/脂肪/饱和脂肪门槛取 GB 28050 声称值；能量/蛋白/碳水为演示值（本品类该三项声称无意义）。

The six coefficient sets are literature-typical, to-be-calibrated (per-ingredient n6 fields in the data file); the GB-claims preset takes sugar/fat/saturates thresholds from GB 28050 claims; energy/protein/carb are demo values (no meaningful claims for this category).

原料下界，未计风味/工艺/包装（BOM 含这些——对照蛋白棒 BOM ¥20–25/kg）。价格锚全部带来源与日期，见下方溯源。

Raw-material lower bound; flavour/process/packaging excluded (compare protein-bar BOM ¥20–25/kg). All price anchors carry source and date — see provenance below.

方法与可复算性Method & Reproducibility

求解器：两阶段单纯形法，Bland 规则防循环，全程 BigInt 精确分数（与原算例 Python fractions 同口径）。影子价格从终局基的对偶读出并与中心差分交叉验证；v0.2 新增导出：检验成本、交替最优检测、RHS 基不变区间、目标系数价格区间（临界点）——全部精确有理数。约束装配数据驱动（G1/G2 共用内核，加原料＝改 JSON 不改代码）。零第三方依赖、零构建步骤。Solver: two-phase simplex with Bland's rule, exact BigInt rationals throughout (same caliber as the original Python-fractions case). Shadow prices are read from the final basis duals and cross-checked with central differences; newly exported in v0.2: reduced costs, alternate-optima detection, RHS basis-stable ranges, and objective-coefficient price ranges (breakpoints) — all exact rationals. Constraint assembly is data-driven (one shared core for G1/G2; new ingredients are JSON edits, not code). Zero third-party dependencies, zero build steps.
